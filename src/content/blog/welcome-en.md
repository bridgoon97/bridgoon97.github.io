This is the first post on the new site.

I'm planning to treat this place as a lightweight notebook — for problems I run into at the intersection of spatial audio, signal processing, and deep learning, along with the occasional failure and reflection.

## About this site

I'm calling the visual style "Studio": minimal, editor-flavored, content-first. Light and dark are both tuned and follow the system by default. The chip in the top right swaps between Chinese and English.

The whole site is built with [Astro](https://astro.build) — pure static output, deployed to GitHub Pages. Blog posts are plain Markdown with a bit of frontmatter for language, date, and tags.

## What I want to write about

- A few engineering pains in head-tracked PSZ systems
- Reading notes on papers (especially audio + diffusion)
- Occasional music-related musings
